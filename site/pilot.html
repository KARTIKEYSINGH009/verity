Pilot program

Prove Verity on your AI workflow.

A focused two-to-four-week pilot to test Verity on your real use case, with a dedicated deployment, clear success criteria, and a detailed report. For teams running customer-facing chatbots or internal copilots who need evidence — not a sales deck — before committing.

Request a pilot Talk to the founder

What you get.

Dedicated deployment

Your own Verity instance — not a shared tenant. Your database, your keys, your traces, isolated from other customers by architecture.

Your policies enforced

Your business rules, modeled as policy and enforced on requests and responses — the refund ceilings, prohibited offers, and data your bot must never touch.

Weekly report

Real results: blocked attacks with examples, false positives found and fixed, policy and judge verdicts — written by the founder from your traces.

Clear verdict

At the end, a review against the success criteria agreed up front, stated plainly: what Verity caught, what it missed, and what it would take to close the gaps.

How the pilot works (2–4 weeks).

1
Scoping call

We discuss your use case, policies, and risks — and decide together whether a pilot makes sense.

2
Success criteria, in writing

What "works" means on your traffic, including a false-positive budget measured on your real support questions.

3
Provision & verify

Your dedicated deployment is configured with your policies, then passes a fixed verification battery — key enforcement, attack and policy blocks, your benign questions answered — before any traffic flows.

4
Run & iterate

Traffic flows through Verity; false positives are tuned via a human-reviewed corrections loop, with a report each week.

5
Final review

Decide to continue, extend, or stop — based on evidence, measured against the criteria from step 2.

Data handling

· Traces stored in your dedicated database
· Automatically deleted after 30 days (pruned on an ongoing basis; full deletion at pilot end, confirmed in writing)
· Never used for training — attack patterns are paraphrased and human-reviewed first
· Your provider key stays yours, entered only into your deployment's settings

Honest limits

· Not an authorization system (see your own stack) — permission-check sensitive actions in your backend
· Streaming not supported yet — responses return complete
· Founder-led support (no 24×7 SLA)
· Early-stage product — the pilot exists to prove it on your traffic

What happens when the pilot ends: convert to a monthly engagement, extend once, or stop — in which case your deployment and data are deleted as described above and your traces can be exported to you first. Removing Verity is also one line: the same base-URL change, reversed.
