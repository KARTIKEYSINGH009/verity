What this demo is — and is not.

Live gateway, mock model

Screening here runs the production guard, classifier and policy code. In mock mode the "model" behind it is simulated, so answers are illustrative — verdicts, signals and latency are real.

Evaluation can be unavailable

If the gateway cannot be reached, the playground says so and shows a local-rules fallback verdict, labeled as such. A fallback is never presented as a live result.

Your inputs here are demo data

Playground traffic is public demo traffic. Never paste credentials, personal data, or production prompts you would not want in a demo log.
