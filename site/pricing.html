The fine print, in plain words.

Subscriptions are billed monthly, in advance.

Frequently asked questions.

What happens when I hit my limit?

Requests stop at your quota — the gateway returns a 429 quota_exceeded response for the rest of the month. Extra usage will be opt-in only, with alerts at 80% and 100% of your quota, so nothing extra is ever charged unless you opt in — and overage pricing comes once usage alerts are live in your dashboard. The free tier has no overage and stops at 1,000 requests.

What counts as a request?

One inbound application request processed through Verity — not every internal model call, retry, or evaluation step. A request Verity blocks still counts, because the screening work was done.

What if the assessment finds nothing?

You pay nothing. If our attacks succeed, you pay the assessment fee and we recommend Verity as the fix.

Do I pay for my model's usage too?

No. Your model-provider bill stays yours — you bring your own key. Verity's price is for the security layer, policy enforcement, traces, and support, never your model spend.

What happens after a pilot?

A conversion discussion at the end of the pilot. If you subscribe within 14 days of the pilot ending, the full ₹1,999 pilot fee is credited against your first subscription invoice, remainder carrying forward.

How does billing work?

Monthly, billed in advance.
