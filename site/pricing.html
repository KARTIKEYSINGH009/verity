Frequently asked questions.

What happens when I hit my limit?

Requests stop at your quota — the gateway returns a 429 quota_exceeded response until the next billing month. Paid extra usage is not available yet, and automatic extra charges are not permitted.

What counts as a request?

One request is one inbound application request processed through Verity — not every internal model call, retry, or evaluation step. A request Verity blocks still counts, because the screening work was done.

What if the assessment finds nothing?

You pay nothing. If an assessment finds nothing material — no successful attacks and no exploitable issue — there is no fee. If our attacks succeed, you pay the assessment fee and we recommend Verity as the fix.

Do I pay for my model's usage too?

No. You bring your own model-provider API key and pay your own model bill. Verity's price is for the security layer, policy enforcement, traces, and support — never for your model spend.

What happens after a pilot?

We sit down for a conversion discussion at the end of the pilot. If you paid ₹1,999 for the pilot and subscribe within 14 days, the full ₹1,999 is credited against your first subscription invoice, with any remainder carrying forward. The first three free pilots are not eligible, because no pilot fee was paid.

How does billing work?

Subscriptions are billed monthly, in advance. Paid plans are arranged with the founder — there is no checkout on this page.
