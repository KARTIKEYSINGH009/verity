Frequently asked questions.

What happens when I hit the free limit?

The gateway returns a 429 quota_exceeded response for the rest of the month — your integration is never silently cut mid-request, and your dashboard shows the month's usage against the limit so it never surprises you.

When will Pro pricing be announced?

When the self-serve live gateway is ready to sell — pricing follows the real cost of running it, not a guess. Pilot customers get Pro access first.

How does pilot pricing work?

A fixed engagement price agreed after the scoping call and written into the pilot criteria — no per-seat or per-request surprises during the pilot itself.

Can I get a dedicated deployment?

Yes — that is the Pilot / Enterprise plan: your own deployment, keys and database. See how pilots run.
