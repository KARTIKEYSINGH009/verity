Rules alone: 4% recall

Heuristic patterns caught 1 of the 25 held-out attacks — they only fire on attacks phrased like known patterns. On novel wording they are a safety net, not a shield — which is why the learned layer exists.

Classifier alone: 84% recall

The trained model generalizes to new phrasings. Per-category recall on this run — injection, impersonation, data theft, multilingual, spacing: 100%; social engineering: 80%. Weakest classes: harmful content (50%) and fraud (67%).

Known limitations

The training set is a prototype baseline (200 examples) and grows through the corrections loop. False-positive rate is 10% on deliberately hard benign prompts. The faithfulness heuristic (the judge’s offline fallback) scores 75% verdict accuracy on an 8-case mini-eval in benchmarks.json (unsupported-answer precision 1.0, recall 0.5); the LLM judge is primary in live mode. No filter can guarantee complete protection — Verity reduces risk and records evidence; it does not eliminate either.

Keep your client. Change the route.

Verity exposes an OpenAI-compatible chat completions endpoint, so the integration is a base-URL swap—not an application rewrite.

  • FastAPI proxy gateway
  • Mock mode works without an API key
  • Business-policy enforcement via policy_context
  • Faithfulness grading via grounding_context
  • Blocked requests return a structured 400: blocked_by_verity_guard / blocked_by_verity_policy
  • Traces are admin-only: /v1/traces needs VERITY_ADMIN_KEY and is disabled when no key is set
  • Public-endpoint abuse controls: 20k-char input cap, per-client rate limit
  • Graceful fallback when judge or classifier is unavailable

Trust, stated plainly.

Verity inspects AI traffic, so its own behavior has to be inspectable too.

Open source (MIT)

Full source, training data, and benchmark scripts are public under the MIT license: github.com/KARTIKEYSINGH009/verity. Found a bypass? That is a contribution — new attacks enter the reviewed corrections loop and make the next model stronger.

Data handling

Three contexts, three distinct answers. Your own deployment (pilot or self-hosted): traces (inputs, outputs, flags, latency) live in your database under your retention — 30 days by default; in live mode, answer text is sent to your chosen judge model under your provider key. A free account on this site: traces made with your API key are stored in this site's database and are visible only to you, in your dashboard. The public playground (no account): requests run against a mock model and are logged to the same database, pruned after 30 days like all traces — but they are attached to no account, appear in no dashboard, and the admin traces endpoint is disabled on this deployment, because traces contain raw prompts. Found a vulnerability? Please report it through GitHub Security Advisories on the repository instead of opening a public issue.

Screening is not authorization

Verity classifies content and enforces declared policy on requests and answers. It is not an authorization system: sensitive actions (refunds, transfers, record changes) must also be permission-checked at your tool and backend boundary. Never let a model answer alone authorize one.

Where Verity fits.

Customer support chatbots

The beachhead: storefront and D2C bots that take refund, discount and account questions — exactly the prompts attackers jailbreak into unauthorized offers. Verity enforces the policies the bot was never supposed to break.

Internal copilots and agents

Tools that read tickets, docs and inboxes ingest untrusted text all day. Screening every role — including tool outputs and retrieved context — catches instructions smuggled into that content before the agent acts on them.

Regulated and high-trust flows

Intake, onboarding and servicing flows where a hallucinated promise is a compliance incident. Grounding checks flag answers that outrun the provided context, and traces give auditors the record.

Frequently asked questions.

What is included in the free plan?

A personal account on this site: your own API key, a dashboard with your requests and traces, and gateway screening (guard, policy checks, grounding evaluation) against a mock model — so you can integrate and evaluate end-to-end. Free keys are limited to 1,000 requests per month, enforced by the gateway.

Can I use my own model provider?

Yes — on a pilot deployment. Verity is OpenAI-compatible and provider-agnostic: during provisioning your deployment is pointed at your provider (OpenAI, Groq, Anthropic-compatible endpoints, self-hosted) with your key, which never passes through any Verity form or chat.

Do you store my data?

It depends on the context, so here is each one. Free account on this site: traces from your API key are stored in the site's database (hosted in Singapore), visible only to you in your dashboard, pruned after 30 days, and never used for training. Public playground (no account): requests are logged and pruned the same way, but attached to no account and shown to no one — the admin traces endpoint is disabled on this deployment. Your own deployment (pilot or self-hosted): traces live in your database, under your retention settings and your control, and never pass through this site.

What are the current limitations?

Streaming responses are not passed through yet. The free tier runs a mock model. Self-serve live deployments (Pro) are in development — today, live traffic means a founder-led pilot. Verity also is not an authorization system: permission-check sensitive actions in your own backend.

Can I get a dedicated deployment?

Yes — that is exactly what a pilot is: your own deployment, keys and database, isolated by architecture, with weekly founder reports and success criteria agreed in writing.
